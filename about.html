Who I am

From Luanda to Portugal.

My name is Nilson Cardoso. I grew up in Luanda, Angola, and moved to Portugal at 17 to study Computer Science and Engineering.

At 18, I started living on my own while continuing my studies. It was an important change that pushed me to become more independent and adapt to a different environment.

Outside technology, I enjoy going to the gym, have an interest in photography, previously practised martial arts and am currently learning to play the piano.
